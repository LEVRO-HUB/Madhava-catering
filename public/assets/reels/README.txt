Real catering reel videos live directly in this folder.

src/data/reels.js is AUTO-GENERATED from whatever video files are here —
do not hand-edit it. After adding, removing or renaming a video file in
this folder, regenerate it:

    node scripts/discover-reels.mjs

Supported extensions: .mp4, .mov, .webm, .m4v
Videos are sorted by the first number found in their filename.
